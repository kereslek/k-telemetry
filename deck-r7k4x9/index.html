RANGE & EDGE BUFFER

A V3 LP only earns fees while price sits inside your chosen range. "OUT at −9.4%" means: if the price falls 9.4%, that position exits its range and earns nothing until price returns or you rebalance. The hatched zones on each bar are your warning margins.

APR TIMEFRAMES (24H / 7D / 30D / SINCE OPEN)

The header switch standardizes every APR on the page to one window so LPs are directly comparable. All values are annualized: "24H · 96%" = yesterday's fee rate would compound to ~96%/yr. On Solana, ◎ marks pool-level APR (position-level needs tx history).

ROI vs FEE APR vs PRICE DRIFT (IL)

ROI = everything (value change + fees) vs what you deposited. Fee APR = fees only. Price drift (IL) ≈ ROI − fees: what price movement alone did to you. Fees high but ROI low → the range is earning hard while price drifts against you.

FEES / $1K / DAY

The fairest cross-LP metric: dollars earned per day per $1,000 deposited — strips out position size and age. The matrix column ◆ marks best-in-class.

LIVE vs RELAY

LIVE · MAINNET = your browser reads the chains directly. LIVE · RELAY = your GitHub repo fetched the data server-side (refreshed ~15 min) — used when your network/antivirus blocks RPC traffic. Both are real on-chain data.

SEISMOGRAPH & RANGE PRESSURE

The wave shows current market agitation: ±%/day is how much the pair typically moves in a day (24h realized volatility); × vs 30d compares it to the monthly baseline — CALM / NORMAL / ELEVATED / STORM. The pressure bar shows the move price must make to leave your range, and how many of the last fourteen two-day windows actually moved that far. That is history, not a forecast — deliberately so. A single "typical" figure would describe a normal distribution these pairs do not obey: daily volatility here implies roughly 6% moves while the largest real two-day move was over 40%. Colour still grades urgency (red = a move that size is common for this pair, green = it would be exceptional). High volatility often raises volume (more fees) but drains buffers faster.

ALERTS

Thresholds are the % price move that takes a position out of range: WARN when 15% or less, CRITICAL when 5% or less (tunable in CONFIG). Arm ◉ ALERTS for browser pings even when the tab is in background.
